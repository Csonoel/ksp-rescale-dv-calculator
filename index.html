How it works
Rescale mods keep surface gravity the same and multiply radius (and usually orbit sizes) by the multiplier s. Orbital speeds scale with √(g·R), so vacuum maneuvers scale by √s. Ascent from Kerbin is modeled as 2300·√s (orbital speed) + 1100·s0.25 (gravity & drag losses), which gives ~3,400 m/s at 1× and ~9,200 m/s at 10× (close to RSS). Stock values come from the community ΔV map. Atmospheric landings assume aerobraking/parachutes. Real numbers depend on your TWR, ascent profile, and mod config — treat these as planning estimates.
